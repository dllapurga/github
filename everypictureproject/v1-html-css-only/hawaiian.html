Kahanuakealohamaikalani

"The breath of love from the heavens"

My hawaiian name was given to me by my Auntie Moani'alaopuna. The name implies a person who carries themselves with warmth and sincerity. It symbolizes grounded compassion, a calm presence, and a life shaped by compassion for others. It expresses a sense of lovee that comes from a sacred source. As I was considered a "miracle baby", this name presents me as a blessing to my family.
