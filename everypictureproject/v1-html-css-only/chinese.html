李愷昀

Li Hoi Wan

李: My mother's maiden name

愷: cheerful, harmonious, kind-hearted, benevolent

昀: soft sunlight, diffused glow of the sun, warmth, radiance

My chinese name was given to me by grandma and grandpa. The name symbolizes a person whose presence naturally brightens those around them with their warm and cheerful personality— an enduring glow that persists through changing seasons. It conveys someone who leads a peaceful and joyful life. The name reflects resilience expressed through softness and strength expressed through kindness and compassion.p>
