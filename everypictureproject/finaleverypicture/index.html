Hi! My name is Derick, but to others I'm...

Li Hoi Wan (李愷昀)

李: My mother's maiden name
愷: cheerful, harmonious, kind-hearted, benevolent
昀: soft sunlight, diffused glow of the sun, warmth, radiance

My Chinese name was given to me by grandma and grandpa. The name symbolizes a person whose presence naturally brightens those around them with their warm and cheerful personality. It conveys someone who leads a peaceful and joyful life. The name reflects resilience expressed through softness and strength expressed through kindness and compassion.

Kahanuakealohamaikalani

The breath of love from the heavens

My Hawaiian name was given to me by my Auntie Moani'alaopuna. The name implies a person who carries themselves with warmth and sincerity. It symbolizes grounded compassion, a calm presence, and a life shaped by aloha for others. It expresses a sense of love that comes from a sacred source. As I was considered a "miracle baby", this name was made to present me as a blessing to my family.

Huni (ᜑᜓᜈᜒ)

A bird's song or melody

This Filipino nickname was given to me by my grandmother. This nickname was given to me because as a child, all I ever did was sing. According to her, I was a cheeful kid always dancing and singing around her house. She would also teach me different songs in Tagalog which led to me learning the language. She used to always call my singing a "gift" and always encouraged me to sing and perform at family events.

Lý Khải Vân

Lý (李): My mother's maiden name
Khải (愷): cheerful, harmonious, kind-hearted, benevolent
Vân (昀): soft sunlight, diffused glow of the sun, warmth, radiance

My mother's side of the family are Chinese from Vietnam, so I was raised with both the Cantonese and Vietnamese cultures and languages. My Vietnamese name is the Sino-Vietnamese variation of my Chinese name. Although the translation doesn't exactly hold the same meaning as the characters, to my Vietnamese speaking relatives, I was and am always Vân. Therefore, this name too is part of my identity.
